What is this based on?

The level uses the share of sentences containing at least one highlighted cue: none = no detected cues; below 35% = light; 35–69% = noticeable; 70% or more = strong. Each sentence counts once, even with overlapping findings. These are demo display thresholds, not a validated influence scale.

Short excerpts can show a high level from just one sentence. AI can miss or misread cues; offline mode only recognizes a small phrase list. Neither mode establishes intentions, truth, or actual reader reactions.
